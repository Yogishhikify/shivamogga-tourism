About Shivamogga

Shivamogga is one of Karnataka's most beautiful districts, famous for waterfalls, forests, trekking destinations, wildlife sanctuaries, rivers and rich cultural heritage.

Popular attractions include Jog Falls, Agumbe, Kodachadri Hills, Kundadri Hills, Tyavarekoppa Safari and Sakrebailu Elephant Camp.

Popular Tourist Places

Jog Falls

India's second-highest plunge waterfall and one of Karnataka's most iconic tourist destinations.

Agumbe

Known for stunning sunsets, rainforest scenery and rich biodiversity.

Kodachadri

A popular trekking destination with breathtaking mountain views.

Kundadri Hills

Famous for sunrise views and the ancient Jain temple.

Sakrebailu Elephant Camp

Watch elephants bathing and learn about elephant conservation.

Tyavarekoppa Safari

A wildlife park featuring lions, tigers, deer and many other animals.
